# Medical and Research Disclaimer

MedRay v2 is experimental software intended only for research, education, software development, and controlled evaluation.

It is **not**:

- a medical device or certified diagnostic system;
- a replacement for a radiologist, physician, or other qualified professional;
- a PACS, RIS, emergency service, or clinical decision system;
- validated for patient care, triage, treatment selection, or autonomous reporting.

Images, overlays, findings, differential suggestions, reports, and model output may be incomplete, misleading, or incorrect. Every result requires independent review using the original clinical data and approved institutional systems.

DICOM de-identification helpers reduce risk but cannot guarantee removal of every identifier, including burned-in pixel data and private tags. Never publish or attach patient data, credentials, or confidential records to an issue or pull request.

Use of this software is at your own risk. The authors and contributors provide no medical, safety, performance, or fitness-for-purpose warranty.
