# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

MedRay is a research and education project. We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and constructive environment for everyone who contributes, asks questions, reviews code, or shares feedback.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific.
- Assume good intent while addressing impact directly.
- Welcome different backgrounds and levels of technical or clinical experience.
- Give actionable feedback focused on the work, not the person.
- Protect privacy: never share patient data, credentials, private model weights, or confidential records.
- Keep clinical and model-performance claims evidence-based and clearly labeled as research claims.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, doxxing, deliberate exposure of private information, sexualized conduct, and knowingly misleading clinical or safety claims are not acceptable.

## Reporting concerns

For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) and do not open a public issue. For other conduct concerns, contact the project maintainers through the repository's private GitHub contact channel and include only the minimum information needed to investigate. Do not attach patient data or secrets.

Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect contributors and users.
